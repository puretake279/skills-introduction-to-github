## About This Game

### Story
October. After school. A gray Fog rolls into the town of Kirigaoka — and anyone who breathes it stops being human within thirty minutes.

Trapped on campus are the five members of the Kirigaoka Girls' High School Airsoft Club. Their advisor, Mr. Onizuka, told them never to touch the locker in the club room. Today, they open it.

*"Objective: everyone lives to see morning. —Operation start!"*

Fight through a shopping street, a subway line, the city hospital and on toward the harbor lab of Helios Pharmaceuticals, where the Fog may have begun. Search for lost family, rescue survivors, and uncover what really happened on the night of the Fog.

Clear Part 1 to unlock Part 2, "Shiomi Seaside Camp," set on a remote island in the middle of summer.

### How to Play
- **Pick two, swap anytime.** Choose two of the five girls for each mission. You control one while your partner backs you up under AI control — tap a button to switch.
- **Run, jump, dodge, shoot.** Push right through hordes of zombies, reload at the right moment, and unleash each character's signature skill.
- **Four kinds of missions:**
  - **Assault** – fight your way through each zone to the exit
  - **Defend** – hold the barricade until time runs out
  - **Sniper** – cover a teammate from a distant rooftop through your scope
  - **BOSS** – face townspeople twisted by the Fog into something much bigger
- **Loot and gear up.** Collect Weapon Crates and Gear Crates from fallen zombies, then kit out your squad for the next fight.

### Features
- **Tag-team combat with a Bond system** – The more missions a pair clears together, the stronger their Bond and the harder they hit. Max out a Bond to unlock a one-of-a-kind Bond weapon for that girl.
- **319 weapons across 7 classes** – Handguns, assault rifles, SMGs, shotguns, sniper rifles, LMGs and launchers, with burn, shock, frost and explosive effects. Fine-tune them with 18 attachments and 11 paint finishes.
- **255 pieces of Gear that show up on your character** – Head, body, legs and accessory slots, each drawn right onto the pixel-art sprite.
- **Five fighters, five playstyles** – Yui's full-auto bursts, Rei's precision crits, Haru's charge, Mio's drone "Pochi," and Nana's explosives. Level them up and spend Skill Points to shape each one.
- **Solo and fixed-team story missions** – Some stages send a single girl in alone, others a set pair, revealing a side of each character you won't see in the main squad.
- **Plenty to master** – Four difficulty levels (Normal / Hard / Very Hard / Nightmare), the Survival mode "Endless Night" with a boss every 5 waves, three stars to earn on every stage, 46 in-game achievements and the Zombie Files bestiary.
- **Play your way** – Gamepad support, fully remappable keys and buttons, autosave, and text in English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

### Meet the Airsoft Club
- **Yui Asahina** – Club President, Assault. Serious and responsible; her motto is "We all go home together."
- **Rei Kurose** – Vice President, Marksman. The new transfer student, top of her year, and deadly with a handgun.
- **Haru Hinata** – Vanguard, LMG. Former softball ace who shoots first and thinks later. Loves croquettes and her little brother.
- **Mio Shirayuki** – Skirmisher, Gadgets. A Discipline Committee member who breaks the rules, backed by her homemade drone, Pochi.
- **Nana Momoi** – Rookie, Heavy Weapons. "Firepower is justice, Senpai!"

- 「女子高生」は英語で "schoolgirls" と書くと性的な連想をされることがあるため、"high school Airsoft Club" "the girls" と書いた。
